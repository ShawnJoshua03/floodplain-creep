Data covers 2018 to 2023. 2016 and 2017 are not used because building detections are unstable.
Building detections can change between years without any real change on the ground.
Building counts are relative estimates from satellite data, not exact counts.
The flood layer shows river flooding only, at a resolution of about 90 m.
The modeled flood zone is wide because shallow depths on a coarse grid count as flooded.
Flood scenarios are simulated. They are not a forecast.
Results are shown by neighborhood. Neighborhoods with too few buildings show "not enough data".
This is not an official warning.
This is not for enforcement against residents. See the misuse notice.
